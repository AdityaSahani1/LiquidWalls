General
Is LiquidWalls completely free?
Yes. LiquidWalls is completely free to download and use with no subscriptions, no premium tiers, and no feature locks. All features described on this site are available to every user from day one.
Which Android versions does LiquidWalls support?
LiquidWalls targets Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. Some features — such as exact alarm scheduling — require Android 12+ and the user to grant the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission. The app detects what your device supports and adapts accordingly.
Does it work on tablets and foldables?
Yes. The app runs on any Android device including tablets and foldables. The crop editor accounts for screen aspect ratios, so you can compose wallpapers for wide displays just as easily as for phones.
What permissions does the app need and why?
LiquidWalls requests only the permissions it genuinely needs:
  • Set Wallpaper — to apply wallpapers to home and lock screen.
  • Read External Storage / Media — to import images and videos from your device.
  • Post Notifications (Android 13+) — to show optional auto-changer status notifications.
  • Schedule Exact Alarm (Android 12+) — for time-precise scheduled wallpaper changes.
  • Request Ignore Battery Optimizations — recommended so background services survive OEM power management.
None of these permissions are used for advertising or data collection.
Does LiquidWalls drain the battery?
Minimal impact. The auto-changer fires using one-shot OS alarms — there is no persistent foreground service polling in the background when not in use. Live video and web wallpapers use Android's native live wallpaper framework, which is hardware-accelerated. The app also has a built-in "skip on low battery" toggle (below 20%) for peace of mind.
Wallpaper Types
What image formats can I import?
LiquidWalls supports JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and GIF (static frame). HEIC files imported from some devices may be supported depending on your Android version's media decoder.
What video formats work for live video wallpapers?
Any format supported by Android's MediaPlayer — including MP4 (H.264/H.265), MKV, and WebM. For smoothest playback, use an H.264 MP4 encoded at a reasonable bitrate with no audio track. The app loops the video automatically.
Can I use a website URL as a live wallpaper?
Yes. Any publicly accessible HTTPS URL or locally-stored HTML file can be used as a web live wallpaper. The WebView renders the page and displays it as your home screen background. JavaScript is enabled, so interactive animations, Three.js scenes, and clock widgets all work.
Will my wallpapers sync across devices?
Store downloads and your account profile sync via Supabase when you're signed in. Local files (images/videos you import from your device) are device-specific and do not upload to the cloud automatically. Use the Export feature to back up your library manually.
Auto-Changer & Scheduling
The auto-changer isn't working. What should I check?
Check the following in order:
  • Confirm the auto-changer is enabled with at least one trigger selected.
  • Make sure you have at least one wallpaper in the selected source collection.
  • On Android 12+, grant Alarms & Reminders permission in Settings → Special App Access.
  • Tap Remove Battery Restriction on the Schedule page and select "Don't optimise" for LiquidWalls.
  • If your library contains only video or web wallpapers and no live wallpaper service is currently active, the changer will skip them. Add at least one image wallpaper as a fallback.
Why do video/web wallpapers not rotate in the auto-changer?
Android requires explicit user action to activate a live wallpaper service the first time — apps cannot silently switch your live wallpaper from the background if a different service is currently active. Once you have manually activated LiquidWalls' Video or Web service (via the Preview screen), subsequent auto-changer swaps of the same type work instantly by just updating the file path. If your source contains a mix of image and live wallpapers, the changer will always fall back to the next available image wallpaper rather than skipping the rotation entirely.
Can I run a schedule and the interval auto-changer at the same time?
Yes — they are completely independent. You can have a schedule that fires at 7 AM with your morning collection, and an interval changer running every 30 minutes throughout the day from a different collection. The last-applied wallpaper tracking is shared, so they stay in sync.
My scheduled wallpaper didn't fire on time on my Xiaomi/Samsung phone.
OEM Android skins (MIUI, One UI, OxygenOS) aggressively kill background apps. You must:
  • Grant exact alarm permission (Settings → Special App Access → Alarms & Reminders).
  • Disable battery optimisation for LiquidWalls (the Schedule page shows a "Battery restriction detected" banner with a direct button if this is needed).
  • On MIUI: also enable "Autostart" for LiquidWalls in Security settings.
After granting these, scheduled changes fire reliably even after the phone has been off recents for hours.
What is Quiet Hours and how does it work?
Quiet Hours is a configurable time window during which the auto-changer and scheduled changes are paused. Set a start and end time (e.g. 23:00–07:00) and no wallpaper swaps will happen overnight. Quiet Hours wraps correctly over midnight — a 23:00–07:00 window works as expected.
Account & Privacy
Do I need an account to use LiquidWalls?
No. All core features — the wallpaper library, auto-changer, schedules, live wallpapers, and AI generation — work without signing in. An account is only required to use the Cloud Store and sync downloads across devices.
How do I delete my account and all my data?
You can delete your account directly inside the app: go to Settings → Profile → Delete Account. If you no longer have access to the app, use the Account Deletion Request form on this website. All personal data is removed within 30 days of your request.
What data does LiquidWalls collect?
Minimal data, only what is needed to run the service:
  • Account users: email address and hashed password (via Supabase).
  • Store activity: which wallpaper packs you've downloaded, stored in your account.
  • Crash reports: anonymous crash logs to help fix bugs (no personal identifiers).
We do not sell data. We do not use advertising identifiers — the AD_ID permission is explicitly blocked in the app manifest. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
Is my password stored securely?
Yes. Authentication is handled entirely by Supabase, which uses bcrypt hashing for passwords. Adisoft never has access to your plaintext password at any point.
Customisation
Can I set different wallpapers for home and lock screen?
Yes (on Android 7.0+). When applying a wallpaper you can choose Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Both. The auto-changer and schedules also have independent target settings. You can also enable Mirror Lock Screen to automatically apply a horizontally-flipped version of your home screen wallpaper to the lock screen for a paired look.
How does the crop editor work?
The crop editor opens when you tap an image wallpaper to preview it. Use pinch-to-zoom and drag to position the image. The crop fills your full screen resolution with no distortion — the wallpaper is composed at your exact screen pixel dimensions. You can also set a background colour fill for images that are narrower than your screen.
Can I dim the wallpaper?
Yes. The Appearance Settings screen has a brightness slider that applies a semi-transparent black overlay when composing the final wallpaper bitmap. This is baked in at apply time, not a system overlay, so there is no persistent accessibility service running.
