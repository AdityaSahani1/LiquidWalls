⚠️ Deletion is permanent and immediate. Once you delete your account, your profile, uploaded wallpapers, and all associated data are removed instantly and cannot be recovered.
📱
Delete your account in the app
1
Open LiquidWalls
Launch the app on your Android device and make sure you're signed in.
2
Go to your Profile
Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Find Delete Account
Scroll down to the bottom of your profile card and tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm deletion
Read the confirmation dialog carefully, then tap Delete my account to confirm. You'll be signed out automatically.
Don't have access to the app? Email us at adisoftcollection@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email address. We'll process the deletion within 30 days.

📦
Local data on your device

Deleting your account removes all server-side data. Data stored locally on your device — downloaded wallpapers, collections, settings, web wallpapers you created, and AI-generated images — remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data manually.

To remove local data: go to Android Settings → Apps → LiquidWalls → Storage → Clear data.
