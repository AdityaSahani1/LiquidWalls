Account & Data Deletion

Learn what data is stored, what gets deleted, and how to delete your account.

📱 How to delete your account

1
Open LiquidWalls and tap the Profile tab.
2
Scroll down to the Delete Account button at the bottom of your profile card.
3
Read the confirmation dialog, then tap Delete my account.
4
Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately. You will be signed out automatically.

🗂 Data deleted vs. retained

Data Action Retention
Profile (username, display name, bio, avatar) Deleted Immediately
Email address & password (auth account) Deleted Immediately
Uploaded wallpapers (images, videos, web) Deleted Immediately
Download history Deleted Immediately
Reports submitted for content Deleted Immediately
Anonymised aggregated download counts Retained Indefinitely (no PII)
Server access logs Retained Up to 90 days
