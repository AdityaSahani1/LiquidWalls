Privacy Policy

Effective: April 26, 2026 App version: 1.0.0 Package: com.adisoft.liquidwalls Contact: adisoftcollection@gmail.com
The short version LiquidWalls is built so your personal data stays on your phone. Adisoft operates no servers that collect personal information. The app has no user accounts, no first-party analytics, and no crash-reporting trackers operated by us. The only third-party service that collects data is Google AdMob (ads). Everything else — your wallpapers, settings, collections, and AI keys — lives exclusively on your device.

1 Data we (Adisoft) collect

Nothing. Adisoft operates no servers that collect personal information. The app has no user accounts, no first-party analytics, and no crash-reporting trackers operated by us. We cannot identify you, track you, or contact you unless you choose to email us.

2 Third-party services and the data they collect

Google AdMob (Advertising)

LiquidWalls displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable device identifier used to personalise and measure ads. You can reset or opt out via Settings → Google → Ads on your device.
  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address by Google, not by LiquidWalls.
  • App activity signals — aggregated, anonymised data used by Google for ad relevance and analytics.

Adisoft does not receive, store, or have access to any AdMob data. It is processed entirely by Google under their Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via device settings.

AI Image Generation (Optional feature)

If you use the optional AI wallpaper generation feature, you supply your own API key from a provider you choose (e.g. OpenAI, Stability AI). That key is stored only on your device in the app's private DataStore — it is never sent to Adisoft or any server other than the AI provider you chose. Your prompts and generated images are governed by the AI provider's own privacy policy.

Google Play In-App Review

After a successful wallpaper apply, the app may ask Google Play to display a rating prompt. Google controls whether the sheet actually appears. Adisoft never sees your rating, review text, or whether you interacted with the prompt.

LiquidWalls Community Store

The app includes a community wallpaper store powered by Supabase (REST) and Cloudinary (CDN). If you choose to upload a wallpaper, only the file you explicitly selected is uploaded — no personal metadata, account information, or device identifiers are attached. Community uploads are publicly visible to all users.

4 What is stored on your device only

  • App settings (theme, accent colour, layout, brightness, quiet hours, auto-changer rules) — saved in Android DataStore inside the app's private sandbox. Cannot be read by other apps.
  • Collections, favourites, and folders — stored in a private SQLite/Room database that other apps cannot access.
  • Downloaded wallpapers — cached in the app's private files directory.
  • Your AI API key — stored in the app's private DataStore, never transmitted to Adisoft.
  • Legal acceptance flag — a boolean value recording that you accepted the legal terms on first launch. Contains no personal information.
  • Backups you export — written only when you explicitly tap Backup; saved to your Downloads folder or forwarded via the share sheet. Adisoft never receives a copy.

5 Network requests initiated by the app

  • Wallpaper store catalogue and downloads — standard HTTPS requests to Supabase and Cloudinary CDN. Only the default OkHttp User-Agent header is sent.
  • Optional uploads — only the file you explicitly selected is uploaded; no personal metadata is attached.
  • AdMob ad requests — handled entirely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • AI generation — only when you tap Generate; sent to the AI provider whose API key you supplied.
The app never uploads your wallpapers, settings, backups, or AI keys unless you explicitly initiate the action.

7 Children

LiquidWalls is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone — including children. The app contains advertising served by Google AdMob; Google's child-directed treatment policies apply to those ad requests.

8 Data retention and deletion

All data the app stores lives on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it — Adisoft holds no copy and has no way to retrieve it.

If you uploaded a wallpaper to the community store and want it removed, email adisoftcollection@gmail.com with the wallpaper name or link and we will delete it within 30 days.
