Privacy Policy

Effective: April 26, 2026 · LiquidWalls v1.0.0 · Adisoft · adisoftcollection@gmail.com

LiquidWalls is built so that your personal data stays on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, stores, and transmits — and why.

1. Data we (Adisoft) collect

Nothing. Adisoft operates no servers that collect personal information. The app has no user accounts, no first-party analytics, and no crash-reporting trackers operated by us.

2. Third-party services and the data they collect

Google AdMob (Advertising)

LiquidWalls displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable device identifier used to personalise and measure ads. Reset or opt out via Settings → Google → Ads on your device.
  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address by Google, not by LiquidWalls.
  • App activity signals — aggregated, anonymised data used by Google for ad relevance and analytics.

Adisoft does not receive, store, or have access to any AdMob data. It is processed entirely by Google under their Privacy Policy.

Pexels (Optional image search)

If you use the Pexels search feature, your search query is sent to api.pexels.com using our public API key. No account or personal identifier is attached. Pexels' privacy policy applies: pexels.com/privacy-policy.

AI Image Generation (Optional)

If you choose to use the AI wallpaper generation feature, you supply your own API key for the AI provider you select (e.g. OpenAI, Stability AI). That key is stored only on your device in the app's private DataStore — it is never sent to Adisoft or any server other than the AI provider you chose. Your prompts and generated images are governed by the AI provider's own privacy policy.

Google Play In-App Review

After a successful wallpaper apply, the app may ask Google Play to display a rating prompt. Google controls whether the sheet actually appears. Adisoft never sees your rating, review text, or whether you interacted with the prompt.

4. What is stored on your device only

  • Settings (theme, accent, layout, brightness, quiet hours, auto-changer rules) — saved in Android DataStore inside the app's private sandbox.
  • Collections, favourites, and folders — stored in a private SQLite database (Room) that other apps cannot read.
  • Downloaded wallpapers — cached in the app's private files directory.
  • Your AI API key — stored in the app's private DataStore, never transmitted to Adisoft.
  • Backups you export — written only when you explicitly tap Backup; saved to your Downloads folder or forwarded to an app you choose via the share sheet.

5. Network requests initiated by the app

  • Wallpaper store catalogue & downloads — standard HTTPS; only the default OkHttp User-Agent header is sent.
  • Optional uploads — only the file you explicitly selected is uploaded; no personal metadata is attached.
  • AdMob ad requests — handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Pexels search — only when you initiate a search.
  • AI generation — only when you tap Generate; sent to the provider whose API key you supplied.
The app never uploads your wallpapers, settings, backups, or AI keys unless you explicitly initiate the action.

6. Permissions explained

  • SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS — apply a wallpaper.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE / POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show a status notification while the auto-changer or a download is active.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — store browsing, optional Pexels search, optional AI generation, AdMob ads, Wi-Fi trigger for auto-changer.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / WAKE_LOCK — restart scheduled wallpaper rotation after a reboot and briefly wake the device when a change is due.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO — only when you pick a file from your gallery or a local folder to use as a wallpaper.

7. Children

LiquidWalls is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. The app contains advertising served by Google AdMob; Google's child-directed treatment policies apply to those ad requests.

8. Data retention and deletion

All data the app stores lives on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Adisoft holds no copy. If you uploaded a wallpaper to the community store and want it removed, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships inside the app and replaces this page in Settings. We will also update the hosted copy at the same URL. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
