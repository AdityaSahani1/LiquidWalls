Wallpaper Types

Three ways to make it yours

LiquidWalls supports three distinct wallpaper types — from crisp static images to fully animated live video and interactive web wallpapers.

Image
🖼️
Static Images

Crisp, high-resolution static wallpapers from your library. Apply with a custom crop, brightness adjustment, and home/lock screen targeting. Instant and battery-friendly.

Video
🎬
Live Video

Looping video wallpapers powered by a native live wallpaper service. Plays silently in the background with zero notification spam or constant foreground service.

Web
🌐
Interactive Web

Render any URL or local HTML page as a live wallpaper. Build particle systems, shader art, or animated dashboards — and see them on your home screen.

Auto-Changer

Fresh wallpaper, automatically

Set it once and forget it. The auto-changer runs silently in the background, rotating through your library on your schedule.

  • Interval rotation — from 15 seconds to 24 hours
  • Unlock, charging, Wi-Fi, screen-off, and shake triggers
  • Respects quiet hours and low battery preferences
  • Separate home screen and lock screen targets
  • Works even after the app is killed or the phone reboots
📅 Exact time scheduling
Smart Scheduling

The right wallpaper
at the right moment

Assign a specific wallpaper or an entire collection to fire at a precise time on selected days of the week. Multiple schedules, all independent.

  • Set individual wallpapers or whole collections
  • Repeating days — pick any combination Mon–Sun
  • Uses exact OS alarms — reliable even on aggressive OEM ROMs
  • Works alongside the interval auto-changer simultaneously
