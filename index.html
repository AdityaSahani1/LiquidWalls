Everything you need

One app for every kind of wallpaper

Static images, live video, and interactive web wallpapers — LiquidWalls handles all three, with tools to create your own.

🏪
Community Store
Browse and download thousands of wallpapers shared by creators. Static images, looping videos, and interactive web wallpapers in one place.
⏱️
Auto Changer
Set wallpapers to rotate automatically — on a timer, a schedule, on device unlock, on Wi-Fi connect, or when you shake the phone.
🤖
AI Image Generation
Generate original wallpapers using OpenAI, Stability AI, or Google Imagen with your own API key. Your prompts never touch our servers.
💻
Web Wallpaper Editor
Write HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that runs as a live wallpaper. Animate, react to touch, or pull in live data — full creative freedom.
📁
Local Folders
Add any folder on your device as a wallpaper source. The Auto Changer can rotate through your own photo library automatically.
💾
Backup & Restore
Export your entire library — settings, collections, downloads, and web wallpapers — to a single .wcz file. Restore it instantly on any device.
🎨
Collections
Organise wallpapers into named collections and share them as portable .wcc files with other LiquidWalls users.
🔒
Private by Design
No analytics SDK, no advertising ID, no crash trackers. Everything stays on your device unless you explicitly upload it to the Store.

AI Generation

Your imagination, rendered in seconds

Type a prompt, pick a provider, and watch your wallpaper appear. The AI never runs on our servers — you bring your own key, your prompts stay private.

  • OpenAI DALL·E — photorealistic and artistic styles
  • Stability AI — precise control with negative prompts
  • Google Imagen / Gemini — high-fidelity detail
  • One-tap AI prompt enhancer for better results
  • Saved directly to your local library, never to our servers

Auto Changer

Your wallpaper changes so you don't have to

Set triggers, schedules, and quiet hours. The Auto Changer runs silently in the background and rotates through any combination of your libraries.

  • Change on timer, unlock, Wi-Fi connect, or device shake
  • Multiple independent schedules (morning, night, weekend)
  • Configurable Quiet Hours — no changes while you sleep
  • Apply to Home screen, Lock screen, or both simultaneously
  • Works with Store wallpapers, local folders, and AI-generated images
Privacy
