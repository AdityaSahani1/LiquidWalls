Cookies & Identifiers

Effective: June 1, 2026 Package: com.adisoft.liquidwalls Contact: adisoftcollection@gmail.com
The short version LiquidWalls is a native Android app — it does not use browser cookies. It stores only the data strictly needed to run the app locally on your device: authentication tokens, your settings, and a thumbnail cache. No advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, no analytics SDKs. A session token is stored only if you create an account; guests have no persistent identifier at all.

1 Cookies in native Android apps

Traditional "cookies" are a web browser technology. Because LiquidWalls is a native Android application, it does not use HTTP cookies in the way a website would. There is no browser cookie banner to accept because there are no tracking or marketing cookies.

What Android apps use instead of cookies are local storage mechanisms built into the Android OS. This page explains every storage mechanism and identifier LiquidWalls uses, what it stores, and why.

3 Authentication tokens (session identifiers)

If you create an account, Supabase Auth issues a JWT access token and a refresh token after login. These are the closest equivalent to a session cookie.

  • Both tokens are stored in Android DataStore, which is encrypted at rest on Android 6.0 (API 23) and above.
  • The access token is a short-lived JWT (typically 1 hour) that is sent to Supabase servers on authenticated API calls to verify your identity.
  • The refresh token is long-lived and is used to obtain a new access token when the current one expires, without requiring you to log in again.
  • Neither token is ever shared with third parties other than Supabase.
  • Tokens are deleted from your device when you log out or delete your account.
  • Guest users (no account) have no session tokens and no persistent identifier.
Supabase Auth: Token issuance and validation is handled by Supabase. See supabase.com/privacy for how Supabase handles authentication data.

5 AI API keys

If you use the AI image generation features, you supply your own API key from the provider of your choice (OpenAI, Stability AI, or Google). This key:

  • Is stored exclusively in Android DataStore on your device — it never leaves your device to LiquidWalls servers.
  • Is sent directly from your device to the AI provider's API over HTTPS when you generate an image or use the AI assistant.
  • Is treated as a credential — it is not logged, displayed in plaintext in the UI, or accessible to other apps.
  • Can be cleared at any time from Settings → AI Settings → clear your key.

6 Legal consent and policy hash

Two small flags are stored in DataStore related to these legal policies:

  • Legal consent flag — a boolean (true/false) recorded when you accept the policies on first launch. Contains no personal information.
  • Policy hash — a SHA-256 hash of the hosted legal page content, used to detect when policies are updated so the App can notify you in-app. No personal data is involved — it is purely a content fingerprint.

Neither value is transmitted to our servers.

8 Android Auto Backup

Android's Auto Backup feature can sync app data to Google's servers. LiquidWalls explicitly opts out of backing up sensitive data:

  • The Room database (liquidwalls.db) is excluded via backup_rules.xml.
  • Downloaded wallpaper files and web bundles are excluded via data_extraction_rules.xml.
  • Auth tokens stored in DataStore are not backed up.

This means your local wallpaper library does not automatically sync to Google Drive. You can create manual backups using the in-app backup feature (Settings → Data & Storage → Export).

9 Your controls

  • Clear cache: The thumbnail cache can be cleared from Settings → Data & Storage → Clear cache, or via Android system settings → Apps → LiquidWalls → Storage → Clear cache.
  • Clear app data: Clearing all app data (Android system settings → Apps → LiquidWalls → Storage → Clear data) removes all DataStore preferences, the Room database, and cached files from your device.
  • Log out: Logging out removes session tokens from your device. Your account and uploaded content on Supabase remain until you delete your account.
  • Delete account: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your Supabase profile, uploaded wallpapers, and all associated server-side data.
  • Revoke folder access: Any local folder permissions granted via Storage Access Framework can be revoked in Android system settings → Privacy → Permission manager, or by removing the folder from LiquidWalls settings.

10 Changes to this policy

We publish updates to this policy on our website first and notify you inside the app — you'll see a banner in Settings → Legal when the hosted version changes. The updated policy also ships with the next app release. Continued use of the App after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

In-app notifications: When this policy is updated, a notification will appear inside LiquidWalls directing you to this page. You can always find the latest version at this URL or in Settings → Legal → Cookies & Identifiers.
