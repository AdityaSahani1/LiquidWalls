Privacy Policy

Effective: June 1, 2026 Package: com.adisoft.liquidwalls Contact: adisoftcollection@gmail.com
The short version LiquidWalls is built to minimise the personal data it handles. Creating an account is optional and only needed for the community Store. If you never sign up, Adisoft collects nothing about you. If you do create an account, your email and profile are stored by Supabase. Everything else — your wallpapers, settings, collections, and AI keys — lives entirely on your device.

1 Who we are

LiquidWalls is an Android wallpaper application developed and operated by Adisoft. The app lets you browse, download, and apply static image, live video, and interactive web-based wallpapers. It also provides community store features, an AI image-generation tool, and a web wallpaper editor.

Contact: adisoftcollection@gmail.com

2 Data we (Adisoft) collect

Optional account only. Creating an account is optional and only needed to access the community Store. If you register, your email address and a securely hashed password are stored by Supabase (our authentication and backend provider) on their servers. If you never create an account, Adisoft collects nothing. The app has no first-party analytics and no crash-reporting trackers operated by Adisoft.

Account registration (optional)

  • Email address — used for authentication, account recovery, and mandatory email verification before your account is active.
  • Password — transmitted over HTTPS and hashed by Supabase's bcrypt-based auth. We never store your plain-text password.
  • Username — a public, unique handle (3–30 characters) you choose. Visible on uploads you share to the community store.

Profile information (optional, account holders only)

  • Display name — a human-readable name shown on your profile.
  • Bio — a short text description. Optional.
  • Avatar image — a photo you select from your device and upload to our storage. Optional.
What we do NOT collect: LiquidWalls contains no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking pixels. We do not collect your device model, OS version, IP address, or location at the application layer. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or any equivalent service.

4 Data sent to our servers

Our backend runs on Supabase. Data is only transmitted when you perform a specific action — nothing is sent automatically in the background.

Authentication (account holders)

  • Email and password during sign-in and sign-up.
  • Email address when requesting a password reset or re-sending a verification email.

Profile updates

  • Display name, bio, and avatar image (avatar uploaded to Cloudinary CDN; URL stored in our database).

Wallpaper uploads (community Store)

  • The wallpaper file (image, video, or web bundle) — uploaded to Cloudinary.
  • Metadata: name, category, tags, aspect ratio, dimensions, file size, and color palette.
  • Your user ID (posted_by) is attached so the community can see who shared it.

Download records

  • When you download a Store wallpaper while logged in, a record containing your user ID and the wallpaper ID is written to our database. Used to compute download counts and power the "Downloaded only" filter.
  • Guests (not logged in) do not have downloads recorded.

Content reports

  • If you submit a content report, we store: wallpaper ID, wallpaper name (optional), reason category, and your description. Reports are anonymous — your account is never linked to a report in our database. No account is required to report.
The app never uploads your local wallpapers, settings, backups, or AI keys unless you explicitly initiate the action.

6 AI features and your prompts

The AI wallpaper generation and web wallpaper AI assistant features are entirely opt-in and require you to supply your own API key from the provider you choose (OpenAI, Stability AI, or Google).

  • Your API key is stored only on your device in Android DataStore. It is never transmitted to LiquidWalls servers.
  • When you generate an image or enhance a prompt, your text is sent directly from your device to the AI provider's API over HTTPS using your key.
  • We do not log, store, or view the prompts you send.
  • Generated images are saved to your device's local storage and, if you choose, to your personal wallpaper library. They are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly share them to the community Store.
  • Each AI provider has its own data handling practices — please review their respective privacy policies linked in Section 5.

7 Wallpaper uploads and community content

The community Store allows logged-in users to share wallpapers. When you upload:

  • The file (image, video, or HTML/CSS/JS bundle) is stored on Cloudinary's CDN and delivered to all app users.
  • Your username is publicly displayed alongside your upload.
  • Metadata (name, category, tags) you provide becomes publicly readable and searchable.
  • You can delete your own uploads at any time from your Profile screen. Deletion removes the record from our database and instructs Cloudinary to destroy the file.

Data export

  • Full backup (.wcz): Exports your Room database, preferences, downloaded store files, web wallpapers, and AI-generated images to a zip file in your Downloads folder. We do not receive a copy.
  • Collection export (.wcc): Exports a collection as a portable JSON file you can share or import elsewhere.

9 Data retention and deletion

All data the app stores locally lives on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

For account holders: your email address, hashed password, and profile data remain on Supabase's servers until you delete your account. Uploaded wallpapers remain on Cloudinary until removed.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly inside the app:

  • Open LiquidWalls → tap Profile → scroll down to Delete Account.
  • This permanently and immediately deletes your profile, uploaded wallpapers, download history, reports, and auth credentials.

Alternatively, email adisoftcollection@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and we will process it within 30 days. See also: Account Deletion page →

11 Your rights and choices

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your email, display name, bio, or avatar directly in the Profile screen.
  • Deletion: Delete individual uploads from your Profile, or delete your full account in-app (Profile → Delete Account) or by emailing us.
  • Portability: Export your library and settings using the built-in backup feature (Settings → Data & Storage → Export).
  • Opt-out of AI features: Simply do not enter an AI API key. No key = no data sent to AI providers.
  • Notifications: Revoke the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission in Android system settings at any time.

To exercise any right, email adisoftcollection@gmail.com.

12 Security

All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth before storage. Auth tokens are stored in Android DataStore which is encrypted at rest on Android 6.0 (API 23) and above. Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on our Supabase database ensure users can only modify their own records. Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password and keep your device's OS up to date.
