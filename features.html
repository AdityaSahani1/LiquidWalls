What's Inside

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

A complete breakdown of every feature in LiquidWalls — organised by category so you can find exactly what you're looking for.

🖼️
Category 1

Wallpaper Library

Core
🖼️
Static Image Wallpapers

Import any JPEG, PNG, or WebP from your device. Apply with full crop control, brightness adjustment, and per-screen targeting.

🎬
Live Video Wallpapers

Import MP4 or other video files and run them as a native live wallpaper service. Loops seamlessly, no battery drain spikes.

🌐
Web & HTML Wallpapers

Enter any URL or drop in a local HTML file. The page renders live as your wallpaper — shaders, animations, dashboards.

📁
Collections

Group wallpapers into named collections. Assign a collection to the auto-changer or schedule and rotate through them automatically.

🔍
Search & Filter

Full-text search across your library with type filters (Image / Video / Web). Find any wallpaper instantly no matter how large your library is.

☁️
Built-In Store

Browse curated wallpaper packs in the integrated store. Preview before downloading. New content added regularly by the Adisoft team.

📂
Folder Import

Point LiquidWalls at any folder on your device and import everything at once. Keeps your device gallery separate from your wallpaper library.

🏷️
Wallpaper Metadata

Each wallpaper stores its name, type, source path, and colour palette. Used by the auto-changer and schedule to apply content correctly.

⏱️
Category 2

Auto-Changer

Background
Automatically rotate wallpapers without touching your phone. The auto-changer runs in the background using precise OS alarms — not a permanent foreground service.
Smart triggers

Change on your terms

Pick one or more triggers and the changer fires whenever the condition is met. Mix and match freely.

  • Interval — from 15 seconds to 24 hours
  • Screen unlock
  • Charging plug-in
  • Screen-off event
  • Wi-Fi connected
  • Device shake (accelerometer-based)
🎯
Source Filtering

Pin the auto-changer to one or more specific collections instead of using your whole library. Perfect for themed rotation.

🔀
Sequential or Shuffle

Step through wallpapers in alphabetical order, or enable shuffle for a random pick each time — never repeating the last one shown.

🏠
Target: Home / Lock / Both

Apply changes to just the home screen, just the lock screen, or both simultaneously. Configurable independently from any schedule.

🌙
Quiet Hours

Pause all automatic changes during a configurable time window — for sleep, meetings, or Do Not Disturb periods.

🔋
Skip on Low Battery

Optionally pause the changer when battery drops below 20%. Automatically resumes when the phone charges above the threshold.

📡
Skip on Mobile Data

Optionally pause rotation when on cellular to prevent store downloads or web wallpaper loading from using your data plan.

📅
Category 3

Custom Scheduling

Time-Based
🕘
Exact Time of Day

Specify hour and minute precisely. The app uses OS exact alarms so the wallpaper fires at the exact second you set — not "sometime near it".

📆
Day-of-Week Selection

Each schedule repeats on selected days — any combination of Mon–Sun. A morning schedule can run on weekdays only, for example.

🗂️
Source: Single or Collection

Assign a specific wallpaper for a fixed look, or a collection to rotate through — each schedule pick remembers where it left off.

🔁
Multiple Simultaneous Schedules

Run as many schedules as you like at once. Morning collection at 7 AM, night collection at 10 PM — each fully independent.

💤
Quiet Hours Compatible

Schedules respect quiet hours — a 3 AM schedule won't fire if quiet hours are active from 23:00 to 07:00.

📱
OEM-Reliable Alarms

Uses setExactAndAllowWhileIdle with receiver-side re-arming. Survives Xiaomi MIUI, Samsung One UI, and OnePlus battery management.

✨
Category 4

Live Wallpapers

Animated
🎬
Video Live Wallpaper Service

A native Android live wallpaper service that plays your video files in a hardware-accelerated loop — no foreground notification required.

🌐
Web Live Wallpaper Service

Renders a WebView as a live wallpaper. Any URL or local HTML file — including Three.js scenes, particle simulations, or clock apps.

⚙️
Live Wallpaper Settings

Configure playback speed, loop behaviour, muting, and viewport settings for web wallpapers — all from a dedicated settings screen.

🔄
Instant Path Swap

When the same live service is already active, changing to another video or web wallpaper swaps the content instantly — no picker, no interruption.

✏️
Web Wallpaper Editor

Built-in code editor for web wallpapers. Write and preview HTML/CSS/JS directly in the app before setting it as your live wallpaper.

📱
One-Tap Preview

Preview any live or image wallpaper full-screen before committing. Includes a mock home screen layout so you can see it in context.

🤖
Category 5

AI Generation

AI-Powered
🎨
Text-to-Image Generation

Describe your ideal wallpaper and generate it with AI. Works for landscapes, abstract art, sci-fi scenes, minimalist patterns, and more.

🖌️
Style Controls

Choose from a variety of artistic styles — photorealistic, anime, oil painting, pixel art, watercolour, and cinematic presets.

📐
Phone-Optimised Aspect Ratios

Generates at portrait aspect ratios designed for phone screens, not square social media crops. Full-bleed coverage from the first save.

💾
Direct Library Import

Generated images go straight into your LiquidWalls library — no extra download step. Apply immediately or save to a collection.

🔄
Regenerate Variations

Generate multiple variations of the same prompt with one tap. Compare them side by side and save your favourites.

📜
Generation History

Every AI generation is stored in your history with its prompt and settings — revisit and re-run any past generation at any time.

🔒
Category 6

Privacy & Account

Secure
🔐
Supabase Authentication

Secure sign-in with email/password via Supabase. Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Your account is protected by industry-standard auth flows.

☁️
Cloud Profile Sync

Your store purchases and settings sync to your account. Reinstall the app or switch devices and your library transfers across.

🗑️
Account Deletion

Delete your account and all associated data at any time — directly from inside the app or via the web form. Full GDPR compliance.

📤
Data Export

Export your entire wallpaper library and collections as a ZIP. Take your data anywhere, no lock-in.

🚫
No Ad Tracking by Default

LiquidWalls blocks ad-ID access in the manifest by default. No fingerprinting, no behavioural ad tracking, no selling of your data.

📲
Granular Notification Control

Per-channel notification settings for store activity, auto-changer events, and service status. Silence exactly what you don't need.
